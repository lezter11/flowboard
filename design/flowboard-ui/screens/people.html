Manager limits

Delegated administration, bounded

Can invite a Member or Viewer to this project.
Can change Member and Viewer grants.
Can resend or revoke pending Member and Viewer invitations.
Cannot grant Manager or change a peer Manager's grant. Only the Owner can.
Cannot edit another Manager or extend authority to another project.
A Manager can leave their own grant. This prototype disables self-removal so the preview does not strand you.

Invitation behavior

An invitation creates a pending grant. The person appears in the roster only after accepting. Revoking a pending invite removes the grant; it does not delete an account.

No email delivery. Resend and revoke update local prototype state only. No message leaves the browser.
Invitees cannot discover sibling projects. Accepting this invitation grants Website Redesign only.

Read-only preview

Switch to Member or Viewer to see the roster without invite, role or removal controls. Pending invitations are hidden from both roles.
