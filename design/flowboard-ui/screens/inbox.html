Notification rules

Keep the signal small

Opening a notification marks it read. It does not mark every notification in the same card thread read.
Deep links are re-authorized on arrival. A link to a project you lost access to shows an access-denied state, not the card.
No email is sent. Delivery, digest frequency and unsubscribe are not implemented in this prototype.
