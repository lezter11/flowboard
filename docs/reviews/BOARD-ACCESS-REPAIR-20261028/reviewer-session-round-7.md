## Why and options

A permission check is a snapshot of who may act at that moment. Flowboard must keep that permission valid while it performs the later operation. A retained database row lock makes ownership transfer wait for an authorized mutation, or makes the mutation see that transfer won first. Checking only before an awaited query leaves a gap. An atomic SQL ownership predicate is an alternative for simple single-query writes, but multi-query receipts and invitations need one coherent transaction.
